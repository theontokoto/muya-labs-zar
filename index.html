MUYA LABS ZAR is an independent creative technology lab turning curiosity and everyday challenges into practical digital tools.

Rooted in operations experience, we pay attention to how people work: where information gets stuck, which tasks keep repeating and what could become easier. We experiment with software, data, design and AI to explore those possibilities.

Our approach is hands-on: build, test, learn and improve. From connecting devices to creating new ways to organise information or express an idea, we’re building tools that give people more room to think, create and deliver.

How_do_I_get_started?
  1. Windows: extract the ZIP, then open Install-Windows.cmd. Keep the supplied app folders together.
  2. Android: send the APK from the Android folder to your phone and install it. Android may ask you to allow installation from that source.
  3. Open the apps, complete the welcome screens, then connect both devices to the same private Wi-Fi or hotspot. For a cable connection, enable USB tethering.
  4. Compare the security ID and enter the pairing code. Approve screen sharing, control or folder access only when you want those features.

This is an early public preview. Performance varies by device and connection. Android screen sharing needs your approval; reconnecting does not bypass the lock screen. iPhone and Mac apps are not included.
